# Terms of Service — Personal Statistics

**Effective date:** October 11, 2026

## 1. Purpose

Personal Statistics (`personal-statistics`) is a personal, owner-operated tool for reading aggregated Google Analytics 4 reports and maintaining historical statistics related to Chrome Web Store extensions. It is not offered as a public commercial service.

## 2. Access and permitted use

The utility may only request Google Analytics data that the authorizing Google account is permitted to view. Access is granted through Google's OAuth consent process using a read-only Google Analytics permission and may be revoked at any time.

Use of the utility must comply with applicable Google API terms and policies.

## 3. Availability and accuracy

The utility is provided for personal reporting on an **as-is, as-available** basis. Reporting results depend on Google's data processing, privacy protections, and service availability. Figures may be delayed, revised, sampled, or incomplete.

## 4. Privacy

Information about access to Google data, its use and storage, and how to revoke access or delete information is provided in the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).

## 5. Independence and contact

For questions about this utility, see the contact information in the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).

Google Analytics and Chrome Web Store are products of Google LLC. Personal Statistics is an independent utility and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
